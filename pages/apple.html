Apple device resources

Use Apple’s own support and device software for updates, backups, recovery, and ownership issues.

Restoring can erase device data. Recovery or DFU mode does not remove Activation Lock or prove ownership. For protected devices, use the original account owner and Apple Support.

Update or restore

Use Finder on current macOS or Apple’s Windows device software. Prefer Update when offered; Restore erases the device. Check backups and connection stability first.
